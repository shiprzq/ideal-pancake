YOUR SPACE. CLEARER DATA PRACTICES.

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Describes this deployed Aster application

Important preview notice. The application’s operator identity and privacy contact have not been provided. A production operator must publish those details, an applicable retention schedule, and a rights-request process. Do not submit sensitive personal information to this preview.

1. Information the application processes

InformationPurpose and storage
Email address, account identifier, and authentication sessionAccount creation and sign-in through Supabase, with optional Google sign-in. Authentication tokens are stored by the authentication client in browser storage. Password handling is delegated to Supabase authentication; do not put passwords in your notes.
Watchlists, progress, personal ratings, notes, shelves, queue, viewing logs, goals, saved searches, and preferencesProvide your tracking features and, for signed-in users, cross-device access. Structured collection/studio payloads are encrypted before database storage; the account vault key is kept in protected authentication profile metadata. Watchlist identifiers are also kept in account profile metadata. This is not a claim that all application, authentication, or hosting data is end-to-end encrypted.
Guest browser identifierA random browser-storage identifier links a guest collection to its database record. Clearing browser storage changes your access to that collection; it does not automatically erase the old server-side record.
Public anime metadata, ratings, trailer identifiers, source links, and reviewer account names/review textProvide discovery, provenance, Smart ratings, and reports. These public records and retrieval times may be cached in the database. They are not authored by Aster.
Connection and technical informationHosting, authentication, source providers, and embedded-content services may process IP addresses, browser/device information, requests, and operational/security logs in accordance with their own practices.

2. External services and disclosures

Vercel hosts the application. Supabase provides authentication and database services. Google sign-in, when used, is mediated by the configured OAuth flow and Design Arena authentication proxy. Server-side catalog/rating requests contact Kitsu, AniList, Jikan/MyAnimeList, AniDB, and Anime-Planet when applicable. Source availability is not guaranteed.

The application loads web fonts from Google Fonts and catalog artwork from the relevant image hosts, which can receive your connection details. Clicking external directories, reviewer profiles, original reviews, or source links leaves Aster and is governed by those sites’ policies. External links are not proof of safe or licensed content.

3. Trailers and optional third-party players

A trailer iframe is not loaded until you choose to load it. Aster uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embedding domain when a verified YouTube trailer identifier is returned. Playing a trailer can still disclose connection/device information and invoke YouTube’s storage, cookies, and policies. You may instead open the provider’s original video page.

4. Browser storage and exported files

Browser storage is used for your guest identifier, authentication sessions, and migration-compatible identifiers. Signing out clears the active authentication session but does not delete your account or saved server-side records. Clearing site data removes local access information and can make guest data inaccessible.

CSV, JSON, and HTML exports are generated in your browser. Exported reports may include your own tracking material or public user reviews, depending on the export you choose. Aster’s generated ratings HTML files contain inline styling, visible watermarks, source links, and no application scripts or tracking pixels. Your browser and operating system control where downloads are saved and with whom you share them.

5. Security and retention

Access tokens are checked for account-level operations, and collection/studio records use authenticated encryption. No system is guaranteed secure. Public catalog metadata, source provenance, rating caches, saved collections, account records, and provider/hosting logs may remain until removed by their respective operator. Short cache-freshness windows are not deletion schedules. This preview does not currently apply an automated end-to-end retention/erasure schedule.

6. Your controls and requests

  • You can edit or remove tracked entries, notes, shelves, viewing logs, preferences, and other items through the available controls.
  • You can export supported collections and reports, sign out, and clear browser site data. These actions are not equivalent to deleting your authentication account or every retained server record.
  • Account-level deletion, data access, correction, restriction, objection, or portability requests may be available under applicable law, but this preview does not yet provide a configured operator contact or an account-erasure interface. A production operator must supply an accessible request channel before collecting production personal data.

7. Children and sensitive data

This preview is not intended to collect information from children below the minimum age allowed by applicable law. Do not provide sensitive personal data, health information, financial information, or private information about other people. An operator must review local age and consent obligations before production use.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the deployment and operator details change. Its effective date identifies the current version. Third-party services publish and control their own privacy terms.
